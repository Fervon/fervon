FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Trace record my screen all the time?

No. It captures lightweight signals — history, active window, clipboard — not continuous video. OCR is optional and off by default.

Is any of my data sent to the cloud?

No servers, no accounts. It runs with your firewall closed — nothing leaves your machine, and you can confirm that yourself.

How is it different from Microsoft Recall?

Recall is tied to “Copilot+ PCs” and captures constantly. Trace runs on any modern PC, captures far less, and filters before writing.

What happened to Rewind / Limitless?

Meta bought it and shut the app down on December 19, 2025. Trace is the independent, truly local successor.

Which platforms does it support?

Windows, macOS and Linux. The waitlist will know which builds land first.
