What Fervon is

The short definition, then the long one.

Fervon is an autonomous software studio

A workshop where the work that used to occupy a whole development team is executed by AI agents directed by a single person. The output is not billable hours: it is products you can buy and tools you can download. The name comes from the Latin fervere —to burn, to boil, to seethe with fervor—, hence the forge soul and the motto: forged red-hot.

  • Own productsFinished software, priced once, no subscription.
  • Open-source toolsMost of the catalogue is free and inspectable.
  • Agent infrastructureThe studio engine is itself a piece of software.
  • Bespoke developmentThe same method applied to another company's problem.

What "autonomous" means

Not unsupervised. Just without a human bottleneck in the execution.

1

Judgement is human

What gets built, what gets accepted and what gets thrown away is decided by a person. That part is not delegated.

2

Execution is the fleet's

Several agents work in parallel on the same repository, each with its own brief.

3

Nothing ships unmeasured

Before a bug is called fixed it has to be reproduced. A green run with no evidence does not count.

4

A panel of judges reviews

Agents applying different lenses try to knock the change down before it gets merged.

What I believe

Four rules that explain almost every decision this studio makes.

Open source by default

If there is no reason to close it, the code gets published. It is easier to trust what you can read.

Local-first and private

Your data stays on your machine. No mandatory cloud and no telemetry by default.

Self-serve, no sales team

Everything is bought and downloaded on your own. No demo, no call and no "leave us your email".

Closed price, paid once

One-time payment on products and a per-project price on commissions. Never by the hour.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Fervon

What does "autonomous software" mean?

That the building work is executed by AI agents running in parallel around the clock, while one person supplies the judgement: what gets built, what gets accepted and what gets thrown away. Autonomous does not mean unsupervised — it means execution no longer depends on someone typing.

What are Fervon's AI agents?

Processes that read the repository, write code, run the tests and review each other. They are launched in fleets, each with its own brief, and a panel of judges applying different lenses has to sign off before anything gets merged.

Is Fervon a development agency?

No. Fervon is an autonomous software studio: it ships its own products and open-source tools. It does take bespoke work, but per project and with scope and price closed before anything starts — never by the hour or by headcount.

What technologies does Fervon use?

TypeScript/Node and React on the front end, Python where it fits, SQLite and Postgres for data, and LLMs both from an API (Claude) and running locally via llama.cpp. Almost everything is designed to run on the user's own machine.

Where can I see Fervon's code?

On GitHub, under the Fervon organization. Most of the tools are open source under a permissive licence, so the "this never leaves your machine" promise can be verified by reading the code instead of taking it on faith.

Where does the name Fervon come from?

From the Latin fervere — to burn, to boil, to seethe with fervor. Hence the studio's forge soul and its motto: forged red-hot.

Got a problem that fits here?

If something in the catalogue helps you, take it: it is one click away and there is no email gate. And if what you need is for the studio to build something for you, tell me about it and I will tell you whether it fits.
